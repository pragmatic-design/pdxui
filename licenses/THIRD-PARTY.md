- **What**: the 4:3 flag drawings of `pdx-flag`, one module per region in
  `packages/ui/src/flag/flags/`, generated by `packages/ui/scripts/gen-flags.mjs` from flag-icons 7.6.0.
  Taken once, not depended on: no package of the repository installs it.
