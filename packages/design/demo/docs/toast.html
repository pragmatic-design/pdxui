Docs Toast

Toast / Notification

Screen-edge notifications. 4 variants, 6 positions, auto-dismiss with progress bar, stacking.

Variants

4 semantic types. Click to spawn a toast in the top-right corner.

Positions

6 screen positions. Toasts stack vertically.

Stacking

Multiple toasts stack with a gap. Click rapidly to see.
